Choosing an electrosurgical unit

An ESU is a clinical decision — and a long-term investment

The generator in your OR affects patient safety, procedure time and operating costs for years. These are the four questions our specialists help you answer before you buy.

Is it safe for the patient?

Return electrode monitoring reduces the risk of patient-plate burns, and automatic bipolar shut-off protects surrounding tissue.

Is the output consistent?

Tissue impedance changes during every cut. Feedback power control keeps the effect predictable from the first incision to the last.

Does it fit my procedures?

Low, medium or high complexity; monopolar, bipolar or both; endoscopy, laparoscopy or oral surgery — power and modes must match.

What is the total cost of ownership?

Reusable accessory options, spare parts, local service partners and factory support make the difference over the equipment's lifetime.

Technology

Engineering that works quietly so you can focus on the patient

Proprietary technologies developed in Ribeirão Preto, Brazil, one of Latin America's leading healthcare technology hubs.

FPA® TechnologyFeedback Power Adjustment

Continuously measures tissue impedance during current flow and adjusts output voltage to keep power delivery constant.

MRGRAPH SystemReturn electrode monitoring

Monitors the contact quality between the dual return electrode (patient plate) and the patient's skin.

PPC® FunctionPower Peak Control

Pulsed mode for monopolar CUT and COAG. Safer, more efficient polyp removal with reduced bleeding risk.

UPEUser Programmed Energy

Programmable pulse pattern with five intensity levels for fine control in delicate procedures.

Simultaneous coagulationTripolar function

Two monopolar outputs (independent cut and coag) plus one bipolar output can be activated at the same time.

Auto Stop BipolarTissue protection

Automatic interruption in Precise and Standard modes to prevent damage to the patient's tissue.

Argon plasma readyAPC compatibility

PRECISION and VET models are compatible with argon plasma coagulation systems.

Smoke evacuator portOR air quality

Auxiliary output for automatic activation of surgical smoke evacuators, supporting your team's safety protocols.

120-preset memoryStandardized workflow

Store up to 120 procedure configurations, with system self-check and error codes on the front panel.

Who we are

Born inside a medical school. Built on 55 years of innovation.

Deltronix was founded in 1970 within the Ribeirão Preto Medical School of the University of São Paulo (USP), one of Latin America's most renowned universities. Our founder, Dr. José Paulin, developed the first electrosurgical unit manufactured in Brazil to meet the university hospital's own needs.

Today we design and manufacture every unit in our own facility in Brazil, in compliance with international quality and safety standards.

  • 1970First electrosurgical unit manufactured in Brazil.

About Deltronix

Does Deltronix manufacture or import its products?

Deltronix is a Brazilian company with more than 55 years of experience in developing and manufacturing electrosurgical equipment. All products are designed and manufactured in our own facility in Brazil, in compliance with international quality and safety standards.

Where is Deltronix present?

Deltronix has an international presence in South America, Central America and Africa through authorized distributors and representatives. Contact our international team for the complete list of authorized distributors in each country.

Products

What types of equipment does Deltronix offer?

Complete electrosurgical solutions for different levels of clinical complexity, providing technology, safety and efficiency in medical, dental and veterinary procedures.

Can the accessories be reused?

Depending on the application, we provide both disposable and reusable accessories, so each customer can choose the most appropriate solution for their clinical and operational needs.

How do I determine which equipment or accessory is right for my procedure?

Tell our commercial team or an authorized distributor which procedures you perform. Based on this, we recommend the most suitable equipment and accessories to ensure safety and clinical efficiency. Request a recommendation.

Safety & certifications

Is the equipment safe for high-complexity procedures?

Yes. Deltronix equipment is manufactured according to international safety and quality standards and is tested and certified by internationally recognized laboratories and institutions.

Which certifications does Deltronix hold?

FDA, ISO 13485, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and the IEC 60601 series (60601-1, 60601-1-2, 60601-2-2).

Is training required to operate the equipment?

Yes. According to international standards, electrosurgical equipment must be operated exclusively by qualified and properly trained healthcare professionals.
